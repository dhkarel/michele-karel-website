Guiding principles

CARES

Five principles guide my approach to geriatric mental health work.

C

Collaboration

Geriatric care depends on interdisciplinary teamwork and strong partnerships.

A

Age-friendly

At the center is what matters most to older adults – their values, goals, and preferences – along with attention to the other components of age-friendly care such as mentation, mobility, and medication.

R

Resilience

Aging entails growth, strengths, and coping informed by years of life experience.

E

Evidence-based

Programs, policy, and practice must be based on the best available scientific evidence.

S

Support for care partners

Family and friend care partners, professionals, and paraprofessional caregivers need the knowledge and skills to be effective care partners with older adults.
