About

Background

I am a board certified clinical geropsychologist. I love working with older adults for many reasons, including being inspired by human resilience; thinking deeply about ethics, autonomy, and interdependence; and working within the systems in which older adults live and receive care – families, teams, community.

I served as a clinical psychologist in the Veterans Health Administration for 30 years, from 1995 to 2025. I began at VA Boston Healthcare System and moved to VA Central Office in 2011. From 2018 until my retirement I was National Mental Health Director for Geriatric Mental Health in the Office of Mental Health.

At VA Boston I provided clinical care to older Veterans in outpatient geriatric mental health clinic and nursing home care settings. I supervised psychology interns and postdoctoral fellows, and directed geropsychology internship and fellowship training. I also collaborated on research projects and served on the hospital’s Ethics and Palliative Care committees.

In my role at VA Central Office I was responsible for developing, implementing, evaluating, and supporting national geriatric mental health programs. That work included national policy and guidance, staff education, clinical and administrative resources, and communities of practice. I managed many interdisciplinary teams of professionals, all working to improve access to and quality of behavioral and mental health services for older Veterans.

I retired from VHA in September 2025. Along with my consultation projects, I enjoy volunteering in my local community, traveling, and spending time in the woods at my home in Vermont.
